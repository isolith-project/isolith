Status: delivered (wave-2 core, P4 STEP export lane). Task t_bdd887b4.
Branch: task/p4m3t02-export-val-1016a (stacked on task/p4m1t03-accuracy-1016a, which

## What this is

A **golden-diff gate** for the STEP *export* lane. It validates what we write to
`.step` against an **independent reader** — the Open CASCADE kernel via a tiny
committed probe (`corpus/tools/occ_probe`, C++ wrapping OCCT `STEPControl_Reader`,
the exact STEP importer FreeCAD uses — and cross-checked live with FreeCAD 1.1) —
and commits the golden expectations so future exporter drift is caught. No number
is ever taken from our own parsers or writers: the gate measures only what the
independent kernel returns.

## The interop finding on real material (why this card exists)

Our P6-M4-T01 faceted export (`crates/oep-geo/tests/golden/box-reference.step`)
is **parsed but NOT transferred** by OCC 7.6 (`ReadFile=Done`,
`NbRootsForTransfer=1`, then `NbShapes=0`) and FreeCAD 1.1 reports the same from
its `Part.insert` (imports nothing). Root cause isolated with minimal STEP files
written from the spec (t1/t2/t3): OCCT's STEPControl_Reader does not route the
exporter's product/representation wiring
(`SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` + `SHAPE_REPRESENTATION_RELATIONSHIP`) to a TopoDS_Shape,
even though the faceted geometry (CARTESIAN_POINT / POLY_LOOP / FACE_OUTER_BOUND /
FACE_SURFACE / CLOSED_SHELL) is standard.

Proof it is the WIRING, not the geometry: the identical faceted box re-wired to
the OCC-transferable AP214 form (`MANIFOLD_SURFACE_SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` +
`SHAPE_DEFINITION_REPRESENTATION` — see `corpus/reference/box-AP214-convention.step`,
generated by the spec-based `corpus/tools/gen_convention_step.py`) transfers in
OCC 7.6 (12 shells / 12 faces / 36 edges / 72 vertices / area 6 / bbox [0,1]^3)
and in FreeCAD 1.1 (`Part.Shape().read` -> Compound, 12 faces).

Classification: the as-committed export row is the **honest `measured` golden**
(transfer=false, with the wiring reason recorded); the conformance reference row
is the **`measured` target** (transfer=true, exact OCC recovery). The gate is
GREEN on the committed fixtures; the exporter's wiring drift is surfaced, not
hidden, and the exact fix target is committed.

## Golden diff gate

- Pair list: `corpus/export-validation-pairs.json` (schema v1; reuses the corpus
  pair-list pattern from P4-M1-T03).
- Golden: `corpus/export-validation-golden.json` (measured on real OCC 7.6 on U1,
  2026-10-16; cross-checked with FreeCAD 1.1 snap).
- Report: `corpus/export-validation-report.json` + `.md` (generated, committed).
- CLI: `PYTHONPATH=python python3 -m oep_tooling.step_export_validation
  --pairs corpus/export-validation-pairs.json --golden
  corpus/export-validation-golden.json --corpus . --out corpus
  --probe corpus/tools/occ_probe --check` -> `gate: GREEN`, exit 0.
- CI-safe: when no independent reader binary is present (e.g. CI container
  without OCCT) every row classifies `reader-unavailable`, never fabricated; the
  pytest suite stays green without OCC (the committed goldens + report document
  the real-material run).

## Reader seams

- `corpus/tools/occ_probe.cpp` + `build_occ_probe.sh`: the OCCT probe (source
  committed, binary is a build artifact, in .gitignore). Needs `libocct-*-dev`
  (Debian/Ubuntu; OCCT 7.6.3 verified).
- `OEP_STEP_READER` env overrides the probe path; an explicitly-set reader that
  does not resolve is an honest `reader-unavailable`, never a silent fallback.
- FreeCAD 1.1 (snap `freecad.cmd`) is the documented app-level cross-check on the
  same kernel; it is NOT the gating path (OCC probe output is deterministic).

## Taxonomy (closed set)

Skips: `reader-unavailable | reader-error | fixture-missing | unparsable | io-error`.
Drift: `transfer-flipped-true | transfer-flipped-false | geometry-differs`.
There is NO `fail` bucket; a crash in one pair never aborts the sweep.

## [uncertain] notes

- "OCC transfers no shape" is standard kernel behavior for this wiring, NOT a
  claim that the file is invalid STEP (it round-trips through our clean-room
  reader and the independent encoder). This card gates on the independent kernel.
- Conformance shell volume reports ~0 (12 planar faces are not a closed solid to
  OCC `BRepGProp`); golden keys on faces/area/bbox and records volume as observed.
- FreeCAD's own box export (1x1x1) reads back as a SOLID (volume 1) — that is
  the analytic-B-Rep form our faceted passthrough is explicitly NOT claiming.
