# Keelworks Viewer Performance: 1M-Triangle Interactive Budget (P6-M5-T03)

Status: Measured
Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-16
Owner: Keelworks program governance, P6-M5 (renderer backend and preview viewer)
Applies to: oep-render seam (P6-M5-T01 decision), the P6-M5-T02 preview viewer,
the P9-M3-T01 headless render worker, and any interactive preview surface on
U1-class hardware.

## What this records

P6-M5-T03 asks one question: can a viewer hold a 1M-triangle scene at an
interactive cadence on U1-class hardware, and what is the measured number?
This document records the measured budget and the mechanism that produced it.
The acceptance test at tests/test_viewer_perf_1m_acceptance.py asserts this
record and the committed benchmark artifacts together, on real material.

A companion ADR (P6-M5/renderer-backend-wgpu-adr.md) already decided the
backend: WGSL/wgpu, offline, headless, shared by previews and P2P render jobs.
This card is the measured acceptance number the backend must meet.

## Budget

"Interactive" for a viewer means the sustained frame cadence stays above a
floor with headroom for camera manipulation, selection, and clipping over the
seam. The budget recorded by this card:

- Mean frame time <= 33.33 ms (a 30 FPS interactive floor), and
- p95 frame time <= 50 ms (bounded spikes; occasional slow frames allowed).

## Measured result (U1, real material)

The committed benchmark (P6-M5/perf/1m-scene) builds a genuine indexed mesh of
1,002,528 triangles (a deterministic 709x709 heightfield grid, 502,681
vertices, 3,007,584 indices), uploads it to the GPU, and renders frame after
frame headless with wgpu over Vulkan: no display, no window, no surface, no
swapchain. Per-frame wall clock is taken from submit until GPU completion.

- Host: U1 (NVIDIA GB10), Vulkan backend, driver family NVIDIA
- Scene: 1,002,528 triangles, rendered at 1280x720 into an offscreen target
- Warmup: 10 frames. Measured: 120 frames.
- min frame time: 0.16 ms
- mean frame time: 0.31 ms
- p95 frame time: 0.52 ms
- max frame time: 0.74 ms
- mean FPS: ~3200
- Budget verdict: BUDGET MET (mean 0.31 ms is ~107x inside the 33.33 ms floor)

Exact numbers are in the committed report P6-M5/perf/1m-scene/
report-1m-triangles.json, generated by the benchmark binary on U1. The
committed frame-proof.png shows the rendered mesh from a real headless run
(50% of pixels lit, so the draw is real and not blank).

## How to reproduce

Inside P6-M5/perf/1m-scene:

  cargo run --release

on a GPU/Vulkan-capable host (U1). The binary regenerates report-1m-triangles.json
and frame-proof.png, prints the measured cadence, and exits nonzero if the mean
or p95 misses the budget. The crate is deliberately standalone (own
[workspace]) and NOT a root workspace member, mirroring the P6-M5-T01 spike,
so the GPU-less CI container does not try to build or run it; the committed
report and proof satisfy the headless test gate in CI.

## Notes and scope

- The measurement is over the same headless wgpu path the oep-render seam wraps
  (P6-M5-T01 section 5). This means the number is the ceiling the seam must not
  make worse; the seam adds per-frame camera, selection, and clipping work,
  which P6-M5-T02 carries and future perf cards can re-measure over the seam
  crate.
- 0.31 ms mean leaves enormous headroom on U1-class hardware. The budget is the
  governing gate, not the observed best; the observed number is recorded so
  regressions (driver, wgpu version, scene growth) fail loudly against a known
  baseline.
- The benchmark renders one draw of an indexed mesh, which is the dominant
  cost shape for tessellated CAD preview geometry. Instanced/assembly overhead
  and P2P render jobs are separate lanes (P6-M4-T02 importer hub, P9).

## Cross-references

- P6-M5-T01: renderer backend WGSL/wgpu offline headless decision (this card's
  backend), and its headless test gate.
- P6-M5-T02: preview viewer carries the interactive seam (camera, selection,
  clipping) over this backend; this card supplies the measured budget it must
  hold.
- P9-M3-T01: headless render worker reuses the same backend; offline jobs are
  latency-tolerant, so this interactive budget is the tighter of the two.
- P6-M2: geometry/math lane feeds the scene the renderer draws.
