## Source of truth (inside this repo, on main)

All geometry and motion are computed by the shipped code paths on main commit
`e2a8ef9`; the test suites back each lane (editor acceptance, drafting goldens,
kinematics residual, mass properties):

- Mechanism frames: `demos/p7-m3-t02/frames/*.ppm` (90 frames at 640x480); the
  motion is solved by `crates/oep-kinematics` (P7-M3-T02), residual asserted
  in `crates/oep-kinematics/tests/kinematics.rs`.
- Drawing sheet: `crates/oep-drafting/tests/golden/bracket-drawing.svg`
  (also `.pdf` and `.oepgeo.json`); byte-locked by
  `crates/oep-drafting/tests/drawing_acceptance.rs`. The `03-drawing-sheet.png`
  on the front door was re-rendered from the post-fix golden (DRAFTING-TITLEBLOCK
  b9c139d) and again for IDENTITY-CONFORMANCE (2026-10-27) from the golden that
  carries the neutral `ISO-` part-number prefix, at the documented 1800x1272 via
  headless Chrome (sha256 `dafe733f3149d659536027e519690f25a2b8f8da91bca4e828e4de0269c0eeb9`);
  the first capture embedded the pre-fix buggy labels (shifted out of their
  cells), which is why it was regenerated.
- Editor viewport pair: produced by the skew-extrude editor demo
  (`demos/editor-sketch-extrude`), attached to kanban card t_0e2a6fa7 as
  `edpart-before.png` / `edpart-after.png` (byte-identical). The live
  recompute / undo / variable-drive behavior is asserted in
  `crates/oep-shell/tests/editor_surface.rs`.

## Rebuilding the captures

- Mechanism: `demos/p7-m3-t02/manifest.json` drives the 90-frame render;
  `scripts/video-render.py` / `frames_raw/` hold the encode pipeline.
- Drawing sheet: re-render `crates/oep-drafting/tests/golden/bracket-drawing.svg`
  with headless Chrome to a PNG (the committed golden is the byte-lock).

## Known quality ceiling (feature, not packaging)

Editor and viewer viewports render at a hardcoded 640x480 with no
anti-aliasing or UI chrome. Marketing-grade screenshots will need a small
renderer feature: configurable viewport resolution, optional grid and axes,
and selection/dimension overlay in the raster. That is a real card
(suggested: MEDIA-RENDERER), not something editing PNGs can fake.
