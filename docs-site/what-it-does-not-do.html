What Isolith does not do

This page is generated from the measured format capability matrix (format-matrix.json) and the product non-goals. It exists so that a team evaluating Isolith can see the boundary as clearly as the capability: what the readers open is a measured claim with a published gate report behind it, and what they do not open is a documented gap in the same table. A reader-only release never claims to be the editor, and the editor does not claim the breadth of a full commercial suite yet.

What the editor is not yet

The editor is the product spine and is young. These product-level gaps are stated on the home page and repeated here so the boundary lives in one honest list:

  • Full CATIA or SolidWorks breadth of workbenches is not claimed.
  • Sheet metal, weldments, mold tooling, CAM toolpaths, and FEA are not shipped (some are planned for later trains; see the roadmap and the workstream notes).
  • Class-A surface styling is a long-term program, not a shipped feature.
  • Native CATPart files are readable; native editing of CATPart is not.
  • The editor is not the 2027.1 GA deliverable; the interop/PDM foundation release is.

The rule behind the boundary

One rule keeps every caveat honest: a capability is only ever claimed at the exact fidelity a measured run verified. If the matrix does not say Isolith can do it, Isolith does not claim it. Gaps are findings, not defects to hide; a documented wall beats invented geometry.
